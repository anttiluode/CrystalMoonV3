Crystal Moon V3

The moon is the light, and the crystal is a lens. In V3 the lens splits the light: blue bends more than red, so every body throws its colours to different places.

The colours do different work. Brightness grows the bodies. Blue grows the fine grain inside them, red melts it smooth.

And now you can see the grain: the bodies are glass, and the lattice they are made of shows inside them, lit by the colours it sorted.

The moon is still a membrane with another world inside. Fly into it to go a level deeper.
